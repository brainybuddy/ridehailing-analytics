## My Recommendation: Use Option 1 or 3

**Option 1** = Best if they're already thinking about market entry
**Option 3** = Best if you're introducing them to the concept

Both are:
- ✅ Short enough to read in 30 seconds
- ✅ Specific about the problem (not generic)
- ✅ Clear CTA (30-min call, specific ask)
- ✅ Professional without being salesy

---

## How to Send:

**Best approach:**
1. Find the right person (CEO, Head of Operations, Head of Strategy)
2. Send during business hours (Tuesday-Thursday, 9 AM-11 AM)
3. Follow up once after 3 days if no response (don't be aggressive)
4. If still no response after 5 days, try another contact at same org

**Who to target:**
- Operations Director
- Strategy Director
- Chief Business Officer
- Founder (if startup)
- NOT: Communications or PR (they'll block you)

---

## Success Metrics for Your Call:

When you get on the call, listen for:
- Are they interested in customer data? (YES = good fit)
- Are they focused only on infrastructure? (Might not be right fit)
- What's their timeline? (ASAP = hot lead)
- Who's the decision-maker? (Need to know for follow-up)

**Goal of call:** Get to "This is interesting, send us a proposal" - NOT "Let's sign today"

---

## If They Push Back:

**"Too expensive"** → "This is an investment. You'll save ₦630M+ in market entry vs traditional customer acquisition. Let's discuss ROI."

**"We already have data"** → "What data? (Listen) This is different because we have verified contacts + behavioral history. Most companies only have one or the other."

**"Not interested in data, just infrastructure"** → "Got it. We also have operational insights on charging placement, driver recruitment, pricing by zone. Which matters most to you?"

**"Talk to our operations team first"** → "Perfect. Who should I loop in? I can send a short brief for you both to review."
