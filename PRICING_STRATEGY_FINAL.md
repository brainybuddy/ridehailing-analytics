## Negotiation Playbook

### When They Say "₦150M is Too High"

**Your Response Flow:**

**Step 1: Anchor to Value**
> "I understand. Let me put this in perspective: this data enabled ₦7.2 billion in revenue. At ₦150M, you're paying just 2% of proven value. What budget range were you considering?"

**Step 2: Listen to Their Counter**
They'll likely say: "We were thinking ₦50M - ₦75M"

**Step 3: Don't Jump to Their Number**
> "I appreciate you sharing that. There's a significant gap between ₦150M and ₦50M-₦75M. Help me understand - is the budget the only concern, or are there other factors?"

**Step 4: Start Descending the Ladder**
> "Let me see what I can do. If you can commit to signing within 60 days, I can offer ₦135M - that's a ₦15M discount right there."

**Step 5: Trade Discounts for Concessions**

Continue with:
- ₦135M → ₦128M (if they pay in full upfront)
- ₦128M → ₦121M (if they provide testimonial and case study)
- ₦121M → ₦120M (final rounding for goodwill)

**Step 6: Present ₦120M as Your Floor**
> "Given our strategic alignment with E-Mobility Africa's mission, I'm authorized to offer ₦120M - our absolute minimum - if you commit to:
> 1. Sign within 60 days
> 2. Pay in full upfront
> 3. Provide testimonial and case study rights
>
> This is 20% off our standard price and represents the best deal we can offer."

**Step 7: If They Still Push Below ₦120M**

If they say "Can we do ₦90M or ₦100M?":

> "I genuinely wish I could, but ₦120M is our absolute floor with maximum discounts. Below that, the economics don't work for us.
>
> Consider this: you're getting ₦685M worth of data collection value for ₦120M - that's 82% savings. At ₦100M, we'd be undervaluing proven ₦7.2B revenue generation.
>
> However, if budget is the constraint, we have two options:
> 1. Start with a 30-day pilot for ₦10M, fully credited to ₦120M if you proceed
> 2. Payment plan: ₦60M now, ₦60M in 90 days
>
> Which works better for you?"

**Step 8: If They Still Won't Budge**

Your secret fallback (ONLY if absolutely necessary):

> "Let me consult with my partners. The absolute lowest I might be able to go is ₦75M, but only if:
> 1. You sign within 30 days (not 60)
> 2. Pay in full immediately
> 3. Provide extensive case study, testimonials, co-marketing rights
> 4. Commit to being our flagship African showcase
> 5. Allow us to publish your results and success metrics
>
> At ₦75M, we're literally at cost. I need to get approval for this. Give me 24 hours."

**Step 9: Never Go Below ₦75M**

If they push below ₦75M:

> "I truly appreciate your interest, but ₦75M is our absolute minimum for the non-exclusive package. Below that, we're better off waiting for other buyers or keeping the data proprietary.
>
> At ₦75M, you're getting:
> - 1% of the ₦7.2B this data generated
> - 89% savings vs building it yourself
> - 2-3 years time saved
>
> This is exceptional value. I can't go lower."

## Psychological Tactics

### 1. Always Anchor High
Start at ₦150M and ₦300M. This makes ₦120M and ₦240M seem like great deals.

### 2. Make Them Work for Discounts
Never give discounts freely. Trade for:
- Speed (sign within 30-60 days)
- Payment terms (full upfront)
- Marketing rights (testimonials, case studies)
- Commitment (long-term partnership)

### 3. Use "Reluctant Concession" Language
- "Let me see what I can do..."
- "I need to check with my partners..."
- "This is highly unusual, but given your mission..."
- "I'm going out on a limb here..."
- "This is the absolute lowest I can go..."

### 4. Create Urgency
- "This pricing is valid for 60 days"
- "Another organization is reviewing the exclusive option"
- "At these prices, data will likely sell within the quarter"
- "Early commitment discount only available this month"

### 5. Emphasize Value, Not Cost
Focus on:
- ₦7.2B revenue generated (proven)
- ₦685M - ₦1.46B savings (vs building)
- 2-3 years time saved
- 40x - 73x ROI potential
- Competitive advantage

Avoid:
- Talking about your costs
- Justifying why you need the money
- Apologizing for the price
- Showing desperation

## Handling Specific Objections

### "That's too expensive for an NGO"
> "I understand budget constraints. However, this data can help you secure ₦5B - ₦10B in funding. Even at ₦150M, that's a 33x - 67x ROI. This is an investment, not an expense. Plus, we can structure payment terms to ease cash flow."

### "We can get free data from government"
> "Government data is typically:
> - Incomplete (our dataset is 95%+ complete)
> - Delayed (months or years old)
> - Unverified (our data enabled ₦7.2B in actual operations)
> - No support or analytics
>
> You're not just buying data - you're buying proven insights, platform, and strategic support."

### "Can we license just Lagos Island or specific areas?"
> "We only license the complete dataset. Partial data doesn't provide the strategic value needed for infrastructure planning or policy decisions. The competitive advantage comes from comprehensive insights."

### "What if we find errors in the data?"
> "We guarantee 95%+ completeness and GPS accuracy <10 meters. If you find systematic issues, we'll work with you to resolve them during the 30-day evaluation period. Plus, the data enabled ₦7.2B in revenue - it's operationally proven."

### "Other vendors quoted us less"
> "What data are they offering?
> - How many records? (ours: 2.5M+)
> - What geographic coverage? (ours: complete Lagos)
> - What time period? (ours: 2 years historical)
> - Is it proven? (ours: enabled ₦7.2B revenue)
> - Do they provide platform and support? (ours: yes)
>
> When you compare apples to apples, our pricing is extremely competitive."
