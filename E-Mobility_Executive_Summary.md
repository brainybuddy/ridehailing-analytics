## The Opportunity

E-Mobility Africa has the chance to leverage **2.5+ million real-world ICE ride-hailing trip records** from Lagos to accelerate electric vehicle adoption across Africa through data-driven decision-making.

This makes it **ideal for EV transition planning** because it shows current mobility market patterns that need to be electrified, real demand hotspots for infrastructure placement, and operational baselines to model EV conversion economics.
