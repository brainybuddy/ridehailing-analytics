## Valuation Methodology

### Method 1: Revenue Multiple Approach

**Context:**
- The operations generated ₦7.2B revenue over 3 years (2022-2025)
- Annual average: ₦2.4B/year
- This data enabled that revenue generation

**Industry Standards for Data Licensing:**
- Technology/SaaS data: 0.5% - 2% of revenue enabled
- Strategic market data: 1% - 5% of revenue enabled
- Proprietary competitive intelligence: 2% - 10% of revenue enabled

**Calculation:**

| Approach | % of Revenue | Valuation |
|----------|--------------|-----------|
| Conservative (0.5%) | 0.5% of ₦7.2B | ₦36M |
| Moderate (1%) | 1% of ₦7.2B | ₦72M |
| Standard (2%) | 2% of ₦7.2B | ₦144M |
| Aggressive (5%) | 5% of ₦7.2B | ₦360M |

**Recommended Range:** ₦72M - ₦144M (1% - 2% multiple)

**Justification:**
- Data is historical (2021-2022) but enabled 3 years of revenue
- Buyer won't generate same ₦7.2B directly, but will use for strategic decisions
- 1-2% is fair for historical but high-value strategic data

- Operations/support staff: ₦50M - ₦100M

- Vehicle partnerships: ₦50M - ₦100M
- **Subtotal:** ₦485M - ₦970M

**Time Value:**
- 2-3 years to collect equivalent data
- Opportunity cost of delayed market entry
- First-mover advantage lost: ₦100M - ₦300M

**Total Cost-to-Recreate:** ₦685M - ₦1.46B

**Recommended Price:** ₦200M - ₦400M (30-60% discount from recreation cost)

**Justification:**
- Buyers save ₦685M+ and 2-3 years by purchasing
- 30-60% discount is standard for historical data vs creating new
- Still massive savings for buyer

### Option 2: Non-Exclusive Licensing Model

**Price:** ₦40M - ₦80M per customer

**Includes:**
- Non-exclusive data access
- Analytics platform (standard tier)
- Documentation and support
- Export rights for internal use
- Annual updates (if available)

**Best For:**
- NGOs like E-Mobility Africa
- Startups and EV companies
- Research institutions (at lower end)
- Multiple buyers (maximize total revenue)

**Revenue Potential:**
- 3-5 customers × ₦50M avg = ₦150M - ₦250M total
- 10 customers × ₦40M = ₦400M total
- Higher total revenue than exclusive model

**Justification:**
- More buyers = more total value
- Each buyer still gets significant value
- Market development benefits all

---

### Option 3: Tiered Access Model

**Basic Tier:** ₦25M - ₦40M
- Raw data only (CSV files)
- No analytics platform
- Basic documentation
- No support
- **Best for:** Organizations with own analytics teams

**Standard Tier:** ₦50M - ₦80M
- Data + analytics platform
- Standard reports and visualizations
- Email support
- Quarterly updates
- **Best for:** Most commercial buyers (recommended)

**Premium Tier:** ₦100M - ₦150M
- Everything in Standard
- Custom analytics development
- Dedicated support
- API access
- Co-development of insights
- **Best for:** Strategic partners, large corporations

**Upfront Payment:** ₦20M - ₦40M

- 5-10% of revenue generated using insights from data

## Market Positioning Strategy

### Pricing Psychology:

**Don't Underprice:**
- Below ₦30M = perceived as low quality/value
- Undermines perceived strategic importance
- Harder to charge more in future

**Don't Overprice:**
- Above ₦200M = limited buyer pool
- May push buyers to collect own data
- Reduces likelihood of multiple sales

**Sweet Spot:** ₦50M - ₦100M
- Signals high value and quality
- Affordable for serious buyers
- Leaves room for negotiation
- Allows volume sales strategy

## Negotiation Framework

### Anchor Price (Initial Ask):
**₦100M** for E-Mobility Africa

**Justification to Buyer:**
- "The operations generated ₦7.2B. We're asking for just 1.4% of that value."
- "It would cost you ₦685M and 2-3 years to recreate this dataset."
- "This data can help you secure ₦5B+ in EV funding - 50x ROI."

### Negotiation Concessions:

**If they push back, offer value-adds instead of discounts:**
- Extended support period (18 months vs 12)
- Additional custom reports
- Co-branding opportunities
- Exclusive access to future data
- Reduced price for Pan-African expansion

**Discount Levers (use sparingly):**
- Early-bird discount: 10-15% if signed within 30 days
- Payment-in-full discount: 10% for upfront payment
- Partnership discount: 15-20% if they provide testimonial, case study, co-marketing
- Volume discount: If they commit to multi-year or multi-city deal

**Floor Price (Walk-Away Minimum):**
- **₦50M** for premium package with full platform
- **₦35M** for data + basic platform, no custom work
- **₦25M** for data only, no platform

Below ₦25M → Not worth it. Better to keep data and sell to multiple buyers.
